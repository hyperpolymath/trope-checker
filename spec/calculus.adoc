= The Trope-Particularity Calculus — a normative core
:revnumber: 0.2 (consolidated; ratified carrier R-2026-07-07, ADR 0004; O5 adopted per ADR 0005)
:revdate: 2026-10-13

[abstract]
This document specifies the type-level discipline of *trope-particularity*: a
graded effect system over a Turing-complete term language, in which each
operation on a property-instance carries a declared *loss-shape* grade, grades
compose by a fixed algebra, and a declared *use-model* yields a decidable
verdict. It pins the loss-shape carrier, names the nine effects of the
vocabulary and gives the six writable ones as typing rules, states the
composition and order laws normatively, fixes the interaction with general
recursion, defines the verdict, states the soundness results with their
firewalls, defines the language-neutral interchange (the Trope IR) so that a
*tropechecker* can be implemented portably, and lists the open obligations.

This is the v0.2 consolidation: it folds the v0.1 text (2026-06-22), the
grade-algebra addendum (2026-06-23) and the two ratification notes of
2026-07-07 into one normative document. Where a law or result is machine-checked
it is cited by its name in the proof developments (§9.3); where it is open it is
listed in §10. The key words *MUST*, *MUST NOT*, *SHALL*, *SHOULD*, *MAY*, and
*OPTIONAL* are to be interpreted as in RFC 2119/8174.

== Scope, and a disambiguation that must not be skipped

The word *trope* carries three distinct meanings across this programme, and
conflating them is itself a modelling error this calculus exists to forbid.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Sense | Meaning here

| trope-the-valuation
| The bottleneck (max–min) grading of a path by its weakest link, with the
  argmin locating the edge to repair. (The *invariant-path* sense.) Used below
  only by analogy, for the verdict witness (§6.2).

| trope-the-residual
| A residual treated as a sum-typed object rather than a scalar error term,
  with a veridicality axis distinguishing faithful from deceptive channels.
  (The *typed-residual* sense.) Reused below as the grade.

| *trope-the-particular*
| A *property-instance*: a property considered as a non-repeatable, situated
  instance — *this* quality, here, borne by *this* bearer. The classical home
  of the notion is trope theory (Williams, Campbell); the calculus needs only
  what every instance ontology supplies — that property-instances are
  particulars — and not the further theses of any one of them (bundle theory,
  one-category ontology). *This is the object of the present calculus.*
|===

A classical trope is _quality borne by a bearer_. This calculus works on a
*provenance-enriched* trope — a property-instance carrying four fields — and
that enrichment is a deliberate departure from the classical notion, recorded
explicitly so that downstream readers do not attribute it to Williams.

[NOTE]
====
*Trope-particularity* (working definition). The _recoverable individuating
content_ of a property-instance: how much of its being-this-one-in-this-situation
survives an operation. It is *graded and use-relative*, not binary and absolute.
Classical particularity is the limiting case where the only admissible verdicts
are "identical" and "not identical"; this calculus refines that to a chain per
coordinate.
====

== The object: property-instances

A *property-instance* (hereafter *trope*) is a record over the *field set*

[stem]
++++
\Phi = \{\, \mathsf{quality},\ \mathsf{bearer},\ \mathsf{context},\ \mathsf{record}\,\}.
++++

The four fields carry, respectively: the situated quality itself; the entity it
is a quality _of_; the situation that individuates it; and an honest provenance
of how the record came to be. A *field-presence-indexed* trope type is written
`Trope[S]` for stem:[S \subseteq \Phi]; the fully-present trope is `Trope[Φ]`.
A trope whose quality has been severed from its bearer is a distinct type,
`FloatingQuality`, with no bearer field. Collapse of a quality to a predicate
yields a non-trope type — the predicate's codomain (typically `Bool`).

Two of the calculus's constructs are the ones that require property-instances
to be particulars rather than universals: *attenuation* (only an instance can
lose fidelity; a universal is exact) and *detaching* (a quality that survives
severance as a free-standing `FloatingQuality` is an instance with no bearer;
severing a universal from its instance leaves the universal, which was never
_this_). Everything else in the calculus would survive a change of ontology;
these two would not.

== The grade: the loss-shape carrier

A grade records what an operation did to a trope. It is *not a scalar*: two
operations can lose the same _amount_ yet differ in _kind_ and in _honesty_.
A grade stem:[g] is a record of three coordinate *kinds*,

[stem]
++++
g \;=\; (\mathsf{fate},\ \mathsf{bond},\ \mathsf{merge}),
++++

where `fate` is itself indexed by the four fields of stem:[\Phi]. A grade
therefore has *six slots* (four fates, one bond, one merge) over *three
coordinate kinds*; both descriptions are used in the stack and mean the same
carrier. Composition (§4) and the order (§5) act *slot-wise*, which is what
keeps the algebra decidable while the term language stays Turing complete (§7).

=== Coordinate 1 — field fate

A *fate* records, per field, what survived. Let stem:[\delta \in \mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty,\top\}]
be a fidelity element, where finite stem:[\delta] is a quantified loss in the
tropical (min-plus) order, stem:[\infty] (`Total`) is total quantified loss,
and stem:[\top] (`Unknown`) is loss of *unknown amount*.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Fate | Reading

| `Present`            | The field survives faithfully. (Chain top.)
| `Attenuated(δ)`      | Survives, fidelity degraded by stem:[\delta]. `Attenuated(0)` is identified with `Present` by normalization (A3, below).
| `Predicated(P)`      | (`quality` only.) Survives *only* as the value of predicate `P` over it — the checkbox.
| `Dropped`            | Withheld entirely. (Honest bottom.)
| `Falsified`          | *Deceptive dual:* a value asserted faithful that is in fact false. (Chain bottom; fails every honest floor. Not writable in source — a lowering fault, §6.)
|===

The fate order is a *complete chain* (R-2026-07-07 (A2); v0.1 had a
`Predicated ∥ Dropped` antichain):

[stem]
++++
\mathsf{Falsified} \sqsubset \mathsf{Dropped} \sqsubset \mathsf{Predicated}(P)
\sqsubset \mathsf{Attenuated}(\top) \sqsubset \mathsf{Attenuated}(\infty)
\sqsubset \cdots \sqsubset \mathsf{Attenuated}(1) \sqsubset \mathsf{Present}.
++++

`Present` is top; `Attenuated(δ)` decreases as stem:[\delta] increases;
`Predicated(P)` sits below every attenuation and above `Dropped` — a checkbox
retains at least as much of the quality as nothing at all. What the v0.1
incomparability protected is preserved: a use-model needing the full quality
rejects both `Predicated(P)` and `Dropped`; a use-model needing only the
predicate accepts `Predicated(P)` but rejects `Dropped`.

[NOTE]
====
*Ratification record (R-2026-07-07, ADR 0004; analysis in
`spec/notes/2026-07-07-l4-boundary-and-atoms.adoc`).* (A1) each deceptive
bottom is a *two-sided* zero of its coordinate's composition — in particular
`Dropped ▷ Falsified = Falsified`, replacing the v0.1 clause
`Dropped ▷ Falsified = Dropped` that let an upstream honest drop erase a
downstream lie and was the sole source of the algebra's non-commutativity.
(A2) `Dropped ⊑ Predicated`, dissolving the antichain. (A3) `Attenuated(0) =
Present` is *imposed by normalization at IR ingest*: validators rewrite
`Attenuated(Q 0)` to `Present` before grading, and composition preserves normal
forms (finite tropical addition reaches 0 only from 0+0), so carrier datatypes
stay as-is and stored and composed grades are normal forms.
====

`Predicated` is well-formed *only* on the `quality` field. A grade asserting
`Predicated` on any other field is malformed (§6).

=== Coordinate 2 — bond integrity

The *bond* records the constitutive quality–bearer tie.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Bond | Reading

| `Intact`   | Bearer present, tie holds. (Top.)
| `Withheld` | Bearer field dropped by projection; tie *recoverable in principle*.
| `Severed`  | Tie cut, quality asserted free-standing; *irrecoverable*. (Honest bottom.)
| `Misbound` | *Deceptive dual:* the quality presented as borne by a bearer that is not in fact its bearer. (Chain bottom; fails every honest floor. Not writable in source — a lowering fault, §6.)
|===

Ordered `Intact ⊐ Withheld ⊐ Severed ⊐ Misbound`. The distinction between
`Withheld` and `Severed` is the whole content of detaching versus
projecting-away-the-bearer: both leave no bearer, but the former is a clean
named drop and the latter a constitutive severance. *This distinction surfaces
in the type, not only the grade* — detaching changes `Trope[S]` to
`FloatingQuality`, so any downstream operation needing a bearer fails to
typecheck. The bond coordinate is *fibred over the bearer's fate* (HC-4): a
grade with the bearer `Present` and the bond `Severed`, or the bearer absent and
the bond `Intact`, is incoherent and unrepresentable at the validation boundary.

=== Coordinate 3 — merge (cardinality and honesty)

The *merge* coordinate records whether plurality was collapsed, and whether
honestly.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Merge | Reading

| `Single`    | No merge; one trope in, one out. (Top.)
| `Fused(τ)`  | Two blended into one, *labelled* by provenance `τ`; plurality recoverable from `τ`.
| `Conflated` | Two merged into one, *unlabelled*; plurality irrecoverable. (Bottom.)
|===

Ordered `Single ⊐ Fused(τ) ⊐ Conflated` by recoverable plurality. `Conflated`
is the *deceptive* collapse — the tag that would reveal the merge is discarded,
so downstream is silently wrong about at least one constituent; `Fused(τ)` is
the *faithful* collapse. This is the faithful/deceptive veridicality axis of
the typed-residual framework, instantiated on cardinality.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*`Conflated` is reachable in the grade carrier but not by well-formed terms.*
The carrier is complete — it has its bottom — because the soundness story and
the use-model order require it as a formal element, and because a front end
that lowers some source operation to an untagged merge must have its output
*named* as `Conflated` when the validator rejects it (§6, §9). But no
well-formed term of the source language produces it: see the conflation
decision in §6.1.
====

=== The unit

The unit grade is

[stem]
++++
\varepsilon \;=\; (\,\lambda \varphi.\,\mathsf{Present},\ \ \mathsf{Intact},\ \ \mathsf{Single}\,),
++++

— full presence on every field, intact bond, no merge. `preserve` introduces it.

=== The nine effects and the nine atom families

The vocabulary (`trope-particularity-workbench`) names *nine* effects. The
calculus realises them as follows. An *atom* is a grade that differs from
stem:[\varepsilon] in exactly one slot; atoms come in nine families, one per
non-unit coordinate value kind, and *every grade factors as a ▷-product of its
single-slot projections* (`EffectAtoms.lean`: `atom_factorization`,
`nine_families`, `family_realized`, `families_disjoint` — this is O1's
carrier-relative half, §10).

[cols="2,1,1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Vocabulary effect | Coordinate | Value | Calculus status | Veridicality

| `p-preserving`  | —     | stem:[\varepsilon] | writable (`preserve`)                     | faithful
| `p-projecting`  | fate / bond | `Dropped` / `Withheld` | writable (`project[S]`); composite of the `drop` atoms | faithful
| `p-collapsing`  | fate  | `Predicated(P)` | writable (`collapse[P]`); composite of `predicate` + `drop` | faithful
| `p-detaching`   | bond  | `Severed`      | writable (`detach`); composite of `sever` + `drop`       | faithful
| `p-attenuating` | fate  | `Attenuated(δ)` | writable (`attenuate[δ]`); the `attenuate` atom          | faithful
| `p-fusing`      | merge | `Fused(τ)`     | writable (`fuse[τ]`); the `fuse` atom                     | faithful
| `p-falsifying`  | fate  | `Falsified`    | *not writable* — validation fault                         | deceptive
| `p-misbinding`  | bond  | `Misbound`     | *not writable* — validation fault                         | deceptive
| `p-conflating`  | merge | `Conflated`    | *not writable* — validation fault (R-2026-06-22)          | deceptive
|===

Two structure theorems fix this table rather than leaving it a list:

* *Honest Generation.* The five atomic moves `attenuate`, `predicate`, `drop`,
  `sever`, `fuse` together with stem:[\varepsilon] generate exactly the honest
  fragment of each coordinate, and never produce a deceptive dual
  (`Trope.Generation.{genFate,genBond,genMerge}`; Agda `TB.Honest.genFate` etc.,
  `honest-closed-▷`).
* *Veridicality Derivation.* The deceptive fragment has exactly three
  inhabitants, one per coordinate kind — `Falsified`, `Misbound`, `Conflated` —
  each the unique deceptive element of its coordinate
  (`Trope.Veridicality.{fate,bond,merge}DeceptiveUnique`; Agda
  `TB.Honest.{fate,bond,merge}DeceptiveUnique`). One cannot deceptively assert
  a loss; the only deceptions are false value, false bearer, false singleness.

So "seven effects" (six writable plus the conflation fault), which earlier
versions of this document used, and "nine effects" (the vocabulary) describe the
same carrier: nine atom families, of which six are writable surface effects
and three are faults. This document uses *nine* throughout.

== Composition and order — the normative laws

=== Composition

Sequencing two effects (apply the second to the residual of the first) composes
grades by stem:[\triangleright], defined slot-wise.

*Fate composition* (per field), stem:[f_1 \triangleright f_2], on the ratified carrier:

[source]
----
Present        ▷ f             = f
f              ▷ Present       = f
Attenuated(δ₁) ▷ Attenuated(δ₂)= Attenuated(δ₁ + δ₂)      -- tropical: losses add; ⊤ absorbs ∞ absorbs finite
Attenuated(_)  ▷ Predicated(P) = Predicated(P)            -- collapse subsumes prior fidelity loss (and symmetrically)
Attenuated(_)  ▷ Dropped       = Dropped                  -- (and symmetrically)
Predicated(_)  ▷ Predicated(Q) = Predicated(Q)            -- predicate of the bit; rare, defined for totality
Predicated(_)  ▷ Dropped       = Dropped                  -- (and symmetrically)
Dropped        ▷ Dropped       = Dropped
Falsified      ▷ _             = Falsified                -- two-sided zero (A1)
_              ▷ Falsified     = Falsified
----

*Bond composition* — meet in `Intact ⊐ Withheld ⊐ Severed`, with `Misbound` a
two-sided zero. *Merge composition* — meet in `Single ⊐ Fused ⊐ Conflated`,
with `Fused(τ₁) ▷ Fused(τ₂) = Fused(τ₁ ⊗ τ₂)` (nested honest merge, label is the
pair) and `Conflated` a two-sided zero.

The laws the grade MUST satisfy, each enforced by the verified developments (§9.3):

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Law

| *L1 (associativity)* | stem:[(g_1 \triangleright g_2)\triangleright g_3 = g_1 \triangleright (g_2 \triangleright g_3)]. (`Trope.Laws.gradeAssoc`; Agda `gmonoid-assoc`; Lean `GradeR.instCommMonoid`.)
| *L2 (unit)*          | stem:[\varepsilon \triangleright g = g = g \triangleright \varepsilon]. (`Trope.Grade.{gradeUnitL,gradeUnitR}`; Agda `gmonoid-unit{L,R}`.)
| *L3 (bottom-absorption)* | Each *deceptive* bottom — `Falsified`, `Misbound`, `Conflated`, and fidelity's `Unknown` — is a *two-sided zero* of its coordinate's composition (R-2026-07-07 (A1)): no honest absorber launders deception, in either composition order. (Lean `FateA.falsified_two_sided_zero`, `GradeR.gBotR_two_sided_zero`; Agda `falsified-absorb{L,R}`, `misbound-absorbL`, `conflated-absorbL`, `deceptive-absorbs-▷`.) The *honest* bottoms (`Dropped`, `Severed`) absorb within the honest fragment, and `Attenuated(⊤)` within the fidelity dimension.
| *L4 (monotonicity)*  | stem:[\triangleright] is monotone in each argument with respect to the retention order stem:[\sqsubseteq] (§5): composing after more loss never yields more retention. *Holds in full on the ratified carrier.* (Idris2 `Trope.Soundness.{fateL4MonoR,fateL4MonoL}`, `Trope.Order.{bondComposeMono,mergeComposeMono}`; Lean `FateA.comp_mono_{right,left}`; Agda `TB.Order.fateL4Mono{R,L}`.) History: strict L4 failed on the v0.1 carrier at exactly the two points (A1)/(A2) amended — `spec/notes/2026-07-07-l4-boundary-and-atoms.adoc`, `L4Monotonicity.lean` (`comp_not_monotone_{right,left}`, retained as theorems about the pre-ratification carrier).
| *L5 (deception infectiousness)* | Once any coordinate is at its deceptive bottom, no composition restores an honest value. A corollary of L3, stated separately because it is the non-compositionality-of-deceptive-channels result and the moral core; it covers `Conflated` (v0.1's statement), `Falsified` and `Misbound` alike.
| *L6 (commutativity)* | stem:[g_1 \triangleright g_2 = g_2 \triangleright g_1]. New at v0.2: on the ratified carrier the grade monoid is *commutative* (Lean `GradeR.instCommMonoid`, `FateA.comp_comm`; Agda `grade-comm`). The v0.1 non-commutativity (`GradeBoundary.grade_mul_not_comm`) was an artefact of the clause (A1) removed. Order-sensitivity, where a producer wants it, MUST come from DAG structure (§9.1), never from stem:[\triangleright].
|===

[NOTE]
====
L1–L2 make stem:[(\text{grades}, \triangleright, \varepsilon)] a monoid, L6 a
commutative one; the effect system is a *graded monad* on the term category
with this monoid as its grade object. L4 is what the verdict subsumption
(§5–§6) and the recursion rule (§7) need. None of L1–L6 is novel as a _shape_;
the content is that this carrier is the right grade object for
trope-particularity, which §10-O1 marks as the open adequacy question.
====

=== The cancellation structure

Three further facts about the carrier are normative because downstream designs
(splitting, rejoining, any cut) must respect them (R-2026-06-23-02..04, as
amended by R-2026-07-07):

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Fact

| *C1 (conicality)* | stem:[g_1 \triangleright g_2 = \varepsilon \Rightarrow g_1 = g_2 = \varepsilon]: nothing lossy composes back to no-loss. (Lean `GradeR.conical`, `FateA.conical`; Agda `grade-conical`.)
| *C2 (the cancellation boundary)* | Cancellation holds *only* on the finite-fidelity core stem:[\mathcal{C}] (the additive submonoid `Q ℕ ≅ (ℕ,+,0)` of the fidelity dimension) and fails on the honest-but-lossy and deceptive tiers alike: an element of the grade monoid is left-cancellative *iff* it is stem:[\varepsilon]. (Lean `Grade.grade_leftCancel_iff`, `GradeR.gradeR_leftCancel_iff`, with the per-element witnesses of `TierTwoComplete.lean`; Agda `TB.Tier.Q-cancelʳ` and the six `*-not-cancel` lemmas.) Consequence: a lossless *split / rejoin* can be cancellative only inside stem:[\mathcal{C}]; any such operator (§10, CO-1) MUST be stated against this boundary.
| *C3 (partition)* | `Dropped`, `Total` (stem:[\infty]), `Severed`, `Predicated`, `Withheld` and `Fused` are *honest* non-cancellative elements; `Falsified`, `Misbound`, `Conflated` and `Unknown` (stem:[\top]) are the *deceptive* ones. Absorbing is not the same as lying. (Lean `dropped_absorbing`, `fidelity_total_honest_not_cancel`; Agda `TB.Tier` classifiers.) Tier preservation under composition is *closure*, not a homomorphism: `tierOf (g ▷ h) = tierOf g ⊓ tierOf h` is false on fate even on the ratified carrier (`TB.Tier.fate-tier-meet-hom-fails`).
|===

=== The tropical factorisation

A nontrivial monoid homomorphism stem:[\pi_R : (\mathrm{GradeR}, \triangleright, \varepsilon) \to (\Delta, +, Q\,0)]
onto the commutative tropical fidelity carrier exists and is
*veridicality-sighted*: `Dropped ↦ ∞` and `Falsified ↦ ⊤`, so the hom is blind
to *which honest* absorber acted but not to *whether the absorber was honest*
(`GradeFactorisationR.lean`: `piR_hom`, `piR_nontrivial`, `piR_surjective`,
`piR_sighted`). The naive hom that reads the attenuation payload off verbatim is
*not* a homomorphism on the ratified carrier (`phiId_not_hom`); the repair
squashes `Unknown` to the deceptive top. On the v0.1 carrier every such hom was
forced to collapse `Dropped` with `Falsified` (`GradeFactorisation.lean`,
`forced_collapse_general`) — a theorem about the pre-ratification carrier only.
This factorisation is a *precondition*, not a recursion result: whether a
fixpoint's grade lives in the commutative part is a CO-1 question (§10).

=== The retention order and use-models

Define stem:[\sqsubseteq] as the *retention order*: stem:[x \sqsubseteq y] means
"stem:[y] retains at least as much particularity as stem:[x]" — i.e. stem:[y] is
higher (less lossy) in every slot. stem:[\sqsubseteq] is the slot-wise product
order; it is decidable because each coordinate is a decidable chain (the
fidelity dimension stem:[\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty,\top\}] is a decidable linear
order with two adjoined tops). (`Trope.Laws.decGradeLte`; reflexivity and
transitivity in `Trope.Coords`/`Trope.Order`, `gradeLteTrans`.)

A *use-model* stem:[U] declares a *floor* stem:[\mathrm{floor}(U)] — the minimum
retained particularity the use requires, possibly specified per slot. The floor
is data the author *declares*; it is the only place a use enters the calculus.
The floor is a partial per-slot demand, not a grade value; HC-4 coherence is
enforced on edge grades, and an author MAY write an incoherent floor (each
demanded slot is then checked independently — harmless, but an author-error
surface, not covered by the unrepresentability claim).

== The six writable effects as typing rules

The typing judgement is

[stem]
++++
\Gamma \vdash e : A \mathbin{!} g
++++

— "stem:[e] produces an stem:[A] with trope-effect grade stem:[g]." Function
types carry latent grade: stem:[A \xrightarrow{g} B] is "applying produces loss
stem:[g]." The structural rules (variable, abstraction, application, `let`,
`fix`) are standard; `let` is where composition enters and `fix` is in §7. The
six *writable* effects and the subsumption rule follow; the three deceptive
duals are not term formers (§6.1).

.T-Preserve
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e : Trope[S] ! g
──────────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ preserve e : Trope[S] ! g ▷ ε
....

.T-Project — drop a named field-set, keep S
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e : Trope[S′] ! g     S ⊆ S′
───────────────────────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ project[S] e : Trope[S] ! g ▷ drop(S′ ∖ S)
....

where `drop(D)` is the grade with `fate(φ)=Dropped` for `φ∈D`, `Present`
otherwise; `bond = Withheld` if `bearer∈D` else `Intact`; `merge = Single`.

.T-Collapse — quality becomes a predicate; everything else withheld
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e : Trope[S] ! g     quality ∈ S     P : Quality → C
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ collapse[P] e : C ! g ▷ predicate(P)
....

where `predicate(P)` has `fate(quality)=Predicated(P)`, all other fields
`Dropped`, `bond = Withheld`, `merge = Single`.

.T-Detach — sever the quality–bearer bond; quality survives intact
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e : Trope[S] ! g     {quality, bearer} ⊆ S
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ detach e : FloatingQuality ! g ▷ sever
....

where `sever` has `fate(quality)=Present`, `fate(bearer)=fate(context)=
fate(record)=Dropped`, `bond = Severed`, `merge = Single`. The `Severed`
bond — not the dropped fields — is what distinguishes detaching from a
projection that drops the same fields; and the type change to `FloatingQuality`
makes the severance structurally visible.

.T-Attenuate — degrade fidelity by δ (δ = ⊤ when the amount is unknown)
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e : A ! g
────────────────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ attenuate[δ] e : A ! g ▷ atten(δ)
....

where `atten(δ)` sets `fate(φ)=Attenuated(δ)` for every field present in `A`,
leaves `bond` and `merge` unchanged.

.T-Fuse — two into one, labelled by provenance τ
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e₁ : A ! g₁     Γ ⊢ e₂ : A ! g₂
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ fuse[τ] e₁ e₂ : A ! (g₁ ⊓ g₂) ▷ label(τ)
....

where `label(τ)` sets `merge = Fused(τ)`, fates `Present` (the labelled blend is
faithful), `bond` the meet of the inputs'. The stem:[\sqcap] meets the two input
grades in the retention order (the fused value is no better than its worse
constituent in any slot). The tag `τ` is *required*: a fusion term with no tag
does not parse (§6.1).

.T-Sub — conservative weakening: you MAY always claim more loss than occurred
[%hardbreaks]
....
Γ ⊢ e : A ! g     g′ ⊑ g
────────────────────────────
Γ ⊢ e : A ! g′
....

[NOTE]
====
T-Sub weakens *downward* in retention only. Claiming *less* loss than occurred
is unsound and has no rule. This one-sidedness is what makes the verdict (§6) a
safe over-approximation — and it is also why the calculus, as it stands,
certifies *sufficiency* (enough retained) and not *discretion* (no more than a
ceiling retained): a declared grade is a lower bound on retention, never an
upper bound. A ceiling verdict needs the dual theorem (§10, O6).
====

== The conflation decision, and the verdict

=== Decision: deception is not a source primitive

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Decision (dated, for the decision-log)

| *R-2026-06-22* | Conflation SHALL NOT be a writable primitive. `fuse[τ]` *requires* a tag; an untagged merge is a syntax error and does not elaborate. `Conflated` exists only as the absorbing bottom of the merge coordinate (§3.3); a well-formed term never produces it. Conflation therefore arises *only* as a *lowering fault*: when a front end lowers a source operation to a merge and fails to supply a tag, the resulting IR is malformed and the validator (§9) rejects it, *naming* the offending node's merge as `Conflated`. The same holds, by construction, for `Falsified` and `Misbound` (the validator rejects them as not writable in the *prevent* profile). The tropechecker is, operationally, a *deception detector at the lowering boundary*.
|===

This is the door-too-small reading: the dangerous state exists in the semantics
but is structurally unreachable by well-formed terms. It makes the moral content
— _do not silently merge particulars, do not misattribute, do not substitute_ —
a validation error rather than a guideline.

[IMPORTANT]
.Normative variant (deception as a verdict-boundary primitive)
====
Implementations MAY instead admit conflation as a writable primitive caught at
the verdict rather than at validation. Exactly three things change, and nothing
else:

. *Add* a rule `T-Conflate : Γ ⊢ e₁ : A ! g₁,  Γ ⊢ e₂ : A ! g₂  ⟹  Γ ⊢ conflate e₁ e₂ : A ! (g₁ ⊓ g₂) ▷ unlabel`, where `unlabel` sets `merge = Conflated`.
. *Relax* T-Fuse so an untagged merge is well-formed and elaborates to `conflate`.
. *Move* the conflation check from the validation boundary (§9) to the verdict (§6.2): a `Conflated` grade is then rejected only when `floor(U)` demands `merge ⊒ Fused`.

The recommended reading (R-2026-06-22) is _prevent_; the variant is _detect_.
An implementation MUST pick one and state which in its conformance profile. The
reference and fast cores ship *prevent*.
====

=== The verdict

The verdict is *not* a typing rule; it is the top-level checking judgement.
Given a closed program with final grade stem:[g] and a use-model stem:[U]:

[stem]
++++
\mathsf{p\text{-}sufficient}(g, U) \;\iff\; \mathrm{floor}(U) \sqsubseteq g.
++++

If the check fails, the verdict is `p-insufficient`, and the report MUST carry a
*witness*: the operation (DAG edge, §9) whose composition first pushed the
running grade below stem:[\mathrm{floor}(U)] in some slot. This witness is
the trope-particularity analogue of the invariant-path argmin — it names the
edge to repair, not merely that repair is needed.

[NOTE]
====
The verdict enters the use-model as a *subsumption against a declared floor*,
which is what keeps the whole construction a type discipline rather than a
runtime monitor. Read dually: effects *produce* graded residuals (a graded
monad), use-models *demand* graded residuals (a graded comonad / coeffect), and
"is the leftover enough" is the comparison of a produced grade against a demanded
grade in one graded structure. That the producing monad and the demanding
comonad form an adjunction whose unit/counit are exactly produce/demand is a
*conjecture* (§10-O3), not established here.
====

== General recursion, and why it does not break the checker

`fix` is what makes the term language Turing complete. Its grade is the
*tightest declaration the rule can certify*: in the retention order, the
*greatest post-fixed point* of the body's grade functional (equivalently, the
least fixed point in the loss order — v0.1's phrasing). The functional
stem:[F(\gamma)] — the body's derived latent grade under the assumption that the
recursive binder has latent grade stem:[\gamma] — is computable, and monotone in
stem:[\gamma] by L4; each coordinate is a complete chain; so Knaster–Tarski gives
stem:[\mathrm{gfp}\,F = \bigsqcup\{\gamma : \gamma \sqsubseteq F(\gamma)\}].

*The rule (T-Fix-Decl).* The author declares stem:[\gamma]; the checker accepts
iff

[stem]
++++
\gamma \;\sqsubseteq\; F(\gamma)
++++

— decidable (stem:[F] is computable on grades, stem:[\sqsubseteq] is
decidable), and no fixed point is ever computed. Kleene iteration and
ω-continuity are not needed: existence by Knaster–Tarski, checking by the
post-fixed-point test, soundness by induction on call depth of a terminating
run (`spec/notes/2026-07-07-co1-operational-model.adoc` §4).

A recursive trope-transformer that can attenuate without bound has fidelity
grade stem:[\top] — and that is *correct*, not a failure: no finite-δ
signature is a post-fixed point, the strongest accepted signatures sit at
`Attenuated(∞)`/`Attenuated(⊤)`, the checker reports stem:[\top], and any
use-model demanding better rejects it.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*Turing-completeness does not break the checker; it means some grades are
stem:[\top], and stem:[\top] is an honest answer.* The checker stays total
because it computes on the grade carrier, never on the term. The undecidability
that Rice's theorem guarantees is discharged honestly into the stem:[\top]
element.
====

*Inference versus checking.* Synthesising the minimal grade of an arbitrary
fixpoint is undecidable. The checker SHALL therefore be *bidirectional*: grades
are *checked* against *declared* loss-signatures at every recursive and effectful
boundary, and *inferred* only through the straight-line, non-recursive fragment.
Declared signatures at the boundaries; inference in between. The T-Fix-Decl side
condition is an obligation of the *front end* at its recursive boundaries; the
IR checker sees only the declared signature.

*Status.* The operational model this section relies on — the core language
λ-trope, its instrumented small-step semantics with tagged values, and observed
retention stem:[\rho] read off the output tag — is DESIGNED
(`spec/notes/2026-07-07-co1-operational-model.adoc`). Its mechanisation is OPEN
(§10, CO-1): Theorem B's `fix` case is NOT-REACHED and not postulated.

== Soundness, and the firewalls

Let stem:[\rho(\text{in}, \text{out})] be the *observed retention* of one
execution: the grade read off by comparing the input and output trope records
(in λ-trope, the output value's tag). The results, with their status:

[cols="1,4,1",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Statement | Status

| *Grade soundness* | If stem:[\Gamma \vdash e : A \mathbin{!} g] then for every well-typed input, stem:[g \sqsubseteq \rho(\text{in}, \text{out})]: the declared grade never claims more retention than the execution delivers (it over-approximates loss). | *Exact lowering:* PROVED (`Trope.Soundness.soundFromExact`). *β/let core:* PROVED, with the grade preserved exactly (Agda `TB.Reduction`, `TB.Substitution.cut-preserves-grade`). *Conservative lowering and `fix`:* OPEN — CO-1 mechanisation (§10).
| *Verdict soundness* (corollary) | If the checker reports `p-sufficient` (i.e. stem:[\mathrm{floor}(U)\sqsubseteq g]) then by transitivity stem:[\mathrm{floor}(U)\sqsubseteq \rho], so the real residual meets the use-model. | PROVED, given grade soundness (`Trope.Soundness.verdictSound`; `subSound` for T-Sub).
|===

[WARNING]
.Three firewalls — state all three, do not let the theorems launder them
====
. *Soundness, not completeness.* The checker MAY be conservative: it MAY report
`p-insufficient` where the true residual would in fact have sufficed. This is the
safe direction and is acceptable. No completeness is claimed.

. *Relative to faithful lowering.* Grade soundness bounds *IR-level* loss by
*IR-level* grades. It does *not* prove that a front end's claim "this source
operation _is_ a `collapse[P]`" is true. *Lowering correctness is a separate,
per-front-end proof obligation* (§10-O2) and is NOT discharged by this theorem.

. *Veridicality is declared and propagated, not checked.* The checker cannot
decide whether a value claimed faithful is in fact false; that needs ground
truth the IR does not carry. What the calculus guarantees instead is L3/L5:
deceptive grades are two-sided zeros, do not compose away, and fail every honest
floor. The precise statement, and the interface a deployment may bind to do
better, is §10-O5.
====

== The portable tropechecker

The tropechecker MUST depend on no source language's syntax or semantics. It
depends only on a language-neutral interchange.

=== The Trope IR

The portable artefact is a *Trope IR*: a labelled DAG.

* *Nodes* are property-instances, each carrying its field-presence over
stem:[\Phi] and its type tag (`Trope[S]`, `FloatingQuality`, or a collapse
codomain).
* *Edges* are effect applications, each carrying its loss-shape grade (§3).
* A distinguished *use-model node* carries stem:[\mathrm{floor}(U)].

The IR's *canonical wire format SHALL be a JSON-schema-validated document*
(`schemas/trope-ir.schema.json`; normative prose in `spec/trope-ir.adoc`,
version 0.2 — R-2026-07-07 changed verdict semantics only, and implementations
MUST accept `0.1` and `0.2` documents and grade both under 0.2 semantics after
normalization). An *OPTIONAL* Nickel authoring contract MAY be offered
front-side; the schema is the trust boundary. The grade algebra —
stem:[\triangleright], stem:[\sqsubseteq], and stem:[\mathrm{floor}] — is
specified in the IR spec itself, language-neutrally, so that two independent
checker implementations agree by construction. Order-sensitivity, where a
producer needs it, lives in the DAG (routing, multi-parent nodes), not in
stem:[\triangleright] (L6).

=== The checker as a pure function

The tropechecker is a *pure function from Trope IR to a verdict object*:
`p-sufficient` / `p-insufficient`, plus the witness edge (§6.2). It performs
normalization (A3), validation (HC-4 coherence; the prevent profile's rejection
of deceptive values), grade composition over the DAG, and the final
floor-subsumption. *It MUST NOT parse, execute, or otherwise depend on source.*

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Property | Consequence

| *Independence* | Own repository, own versioned IR schema, own conformance suite. The Turing-complete language (Haec) is the *first* front end that lowers to the IR — dogfooding without coupling.
| *Multi-producer* | A static analyser for an *existing* language MAY emit Trope IR for code it did not author (e.g. lowering a dataframe pipeline's operations to `project` / `collapse` / `fuse` edges). The same checker then trope-checks it. The store (`vocarium`) and the query layer (`hermeneia`) are producers of this kind.
| *Honest trust boundary* | Front-end grade annotations are *claims*. The checker validates the *composition* of claims; it cannot validate that a source operation truly *is* a given effect (O2) or that a claimed-faithful value is true (O5).
|===

== Provenance of the ideas (so nothing is overclaimed)

Graded monads, coeffects, and effect rows are established machinery
(Katsumata; Gaboardi; Petricek, Orchard, Mycroft; Uustalu). Property-instances
as particulars are established metaphysics (Williams; Campbell) and established
applied ontology (UFO's moments, DOLCE's qualities, BFO's specifically dependent
continuants). The graded-monad-for-loss reading is the echo-types line. What is
assembled *here* — a graded effect discipline tracking *which kind* of
particularity-loss an operation inflicts on a provenance-enriched
property-instance, with the three deceptive duals as faults rather than moves,
checked against a use-model coeffect demand, lowered to a language-neutral IR —
is, as a *named combination*, the contribution. The atoms are mostly already
named; the unclaimed ground is the unification, the three-coordinate loss-shape
grade, and the structure theorems (§3.5, §4.2) that fix it.
