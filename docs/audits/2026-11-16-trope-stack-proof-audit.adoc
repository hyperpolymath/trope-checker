Proof audit · 16 November 2026

Proof architecture and claim faithfulness across trope-checker, haec, hermeneia, vocarium, the particularity workbench and mathesis

The design is right and the central records are honest; the machinery that turns evidence into grades has three holes — unresolved declaration names, unverified receipt commits, and a collapsed top rung — and the prose that sits beside the proofs has drifted where nothing gates it.

[[1-are-the-proofs-correctly-designed]]
== 1. Are the proofs correctly designed?

*The architecture is sound in its division of labour and weak at its joins.*

=== What is sound

*Prover roles are assigned on principle and the assignment is enforced, not just declared.* Agda is the normative kernel (trope-checker ADR 0005 D4, `docs/decisions/0005-v0.2-consolidation-o5-and-prover-roles.adoc:61-68`), Lean and Rocq are recheck legs, and Idris2 is an executable engineering restatement held off the ladder. That last point is the single cleanest structural result in this audit: Idris2 appears in `verification/pwg.json` only under `reference.idris2`, never under `legs`, and `scripts/pwg-derive.sh:99-106` reads only `.legs.agda`, `.legs.rocq`, `.legs.lean`. No Idris2 evidence can raise or lower any grade, which is exactly what PROOF-POLICY §8.1 requires, and the published 24/17/7 census together with the "three ungraded rows are Idris2-only" narrative reconcile with the ledger exactly.

*Grades are derived, not asserted, and this is mechanically guaranteed.* No theorem record in `pwg.json` carries a `pwg`, `grade` or `PWG` key; the string `PWG-` does not occur in the file at all. `pwg-check.sh` enforces that, and byte-matches the derived census against the published line at `docs/status/PROOF-STATUS.adoc:296`. §8.4 rule 2 and rule 7 are implemented as designed.

*The repo boundary is drawn in the right place and respected downstream.* The grade algebra lives in trope-checker; haec, hermeneia and vocarium do not re-implement it. All three identify their entire `verification/proofs/` tree as the unmodified mint template, refuse to count it as evidence, and publish a census of 0 done of 7 with its denominator. That is the doctrine-10 ethos working as intended, and it is the strongest documentary result in the whole stack.

*The two-carrier discipline is right.* Pre-ratification and ratified carriers coexist, with the historical theorems retained as history rather than deleted (doctrine 7), and the central records police the distinction — the C3 row demotes `dropped_absorbing` precisely on that ground.

*mathesis's catalogue design is correct and structurally prevents the failure it exists to prevent.* A claim row pins a receipt by SHA-256, the receipt is fetched at `source_commit`, and it is handed to a canon reader pinned by hash (`deed_lint.js` at standards `e5a39d7` hashes to `37ff85be…`, exactly the `READER.sha256` pin at `claims-registry.mjs:69`). The reader _derives_ `pwg` from the recheck legs and refuses any receipt whose recorded `:pwg` disagrees. mathesis therefore cannot assign a grade even by mistake — it catalogues, deriving, which is what §8.5 requires.

=== What is structurally weak

*(a) The one field that links a grade to a theorem is read by no machine.* `grep -n declarations scripts/pwg-check.sh scripts/pwg-derive.sh` returns nothing: the string does not occur in either script. The receipts attest that `All.agda` and `Smoke.agda` typecheck; they attest nothing about whether the named declarations are the ones that did. Thirteen of the ninety-five declaration strings in the ledger do not resolve to any declaration spelled that way in the source, and `bash scripts/pwg-check.sh` exits 0 and prints PASS on that tree. This is the deepest architectural defect, because it is the exact seam between the evidence layer and the proofs.

*(b) The top rung of the ladder is degenerate.* The `"2ii"` else-branch at `pwg-derive.sh:113-115` is unreachable: arriving there already entails all three of its conjuncts (`counts()` at :93 and `pwg3_receipt` at :103 are strictly stronger). PWG-1 is therefore granted the moment PWG-2ii is reached, on zero additional evidence, against §8.2's "PWG-2ii, *plus* …". Confirmed behaviourally across four perturbed ledgers: the only values ever emitted were `1`, `3`, `not-applicable` and `ungraded` — never `2ii`. The published `PWG-2ii 0` is a structural certainty, not a measurement.

*(c) Receipt freshness is a hand-written string.* `usable_receipt` (`pwg-derive.sh:64`) tests only `state == "current"`, `result == "pass"` and a 40-hex _shape_. No receipt commit is ever compared with the ledger's own `.commit` or with any per-theorem revision, although §8.2's PWG-1 requires the run to be "on the exact source commit". Planting `ffff…` and `0000…` commits on L1 yielded `pwg=1`. §8.4 rule 4's anti-carry-forward rule is implemented nowhere, and the sole freshness signal is precisely the kind of hand-written assertion that rule 2 exists to remove.

*(d) Token-versus-obligation-title is a judgement call with nothing structural behind it, and it is applied inconsistently.* Where the Agda covers less than the obligation's title, the ledger sometimes says `OPEN` and drops the row to ungraded (retention-order: "Agda proves reflexivity only"; tier2-membership: the exhaustive step is missing) and sometimes says `PROVEN` and publishes PWG-3 while its own note concedes the shortfall (L4 "in full"; A2 "chain"). Same situation, opposite verdicts, no rule.

*(e) The estate has two incompatible receipt formats and the seam is unwired.* trope-checker's pipeline emits JSON with uppercase tokens, a git-tree `inputs_digest` and bare axiom name lists. The canon reader that mathesis pins consumes an §8.5.1 praxis-deed with lowercase §5 vocabulary, a per-file inputs manifest with recomputed SHA-256, axiom dispositions, and `route source` for Agda legs. Neither is wrong in itself; what is wrong architecturally is that the estate's producer cannot feed the estate's consumer, and nothing in either repo's plan owns closing it (doctrine 8).

*(f) Package-local prose is ungated and is where the drift lives.* The central records — `PROOF-STATUS.adoc`, the root `README.adoc`, the ledger's own notes — are careful and, on the Lean side, accurate down to the direction of a disequality. The drift is concentrated in the least-maintained surfaces: `grade-factorisation/README.adoc` and `REPORT.adoc`, `grade-boundary/README.adoc`, `INTERFACE-REPORT.adoc`, an `L4Monotonicity.lean` module docstring, and the workbench's research programme. Every one of those sits _next to_ a proof and reads as authoritative. The repo has a single-source-of-truth discipline for the ledger and none for the documents beside the code.

*(g) One gate lane is theatre.* `just aspect` (`Justfile:151-161`) has an empty mint-template body and prints "Aspect tests passed!"; `test-all` (:203) depends on it. `tests/aspect_tests.sh`, the only local Idris2 banned-construct scan, is invoked by nothing, and `CHANGELOG.adoc:82-85` describes hardening it as though it were live. Silent green in a path the repo treats as a gate (doctrine 3 and 8).

*(h) mathesis's own claims validator is the one of its three that is not closed.* `validate()` (`claims-registry.mjs:86`) checks presence and type of ten fields and never rejects an unknown field, while `bridges.mjs:58` and `theory.mjs:38` do exactly that. A row carrying `pwg:'PWG-1'`, `token:'proven'` and `nonsense:42` was accepted. The ADR's stated defence against an asserted grade is absent from the validator that handles graded claims.

[[2-are-the-proofs-correct]]
== 2. Are the proofs correct?

*What can be concluded without a kernel.* Existence, hygiene and profile — read off declarations:

* trope-checker Agda (`verification/proofs/agda/theorem-b/`): every headline named in `Smoke.agda` exists with the type its `using` clause implies; no `postulate`, hole, `TERMINATING` or `trustMe` anywhere in the tree; all nine modules and the `.agda-lib` carry `--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible`, so the §2.1 profile claim is supported as written.
* trope-checker Idris2: all eight proof modules are `%default total` and exhaustive by clause; a grep over all sixteen `.idr` files is clean of `believe_me`, `really_believe_me`, `assert_total`, `assert_smaller`, `unsafePerformIO`, `idris_crash` and `postulate`.
* trope-checker Lean: 157 distinct declared names, and *every* Lean identifier cited in prose exists in the source — no phantom theorem on that side.
* mathesis: every Agda declaration named in THY-1 and APP-1..APP-4 exists at the stated module path; all seven Agda, two Rocq and three Lean digests in `verification/receipts/theory_praxis.deed` match the files on disk byte for byte; the receipt's own SHA-256 matches what `claims.jsonl` pins; `034f215` is a real ancestor of HEAD with a `verification/` tree identical to `a1388fc`'s. `coq/Cohort.v` carries 82 named theorems (matching "all 82") and `lean4/Cohort.lean` 87 source-level `theorem`/`lemma` declarations, with no `sorry`, `Admitted` or `Axiom` in either — the recheck legs are genuine parallel re-statements, not stubs.
* haec, hermeneia, vocarium: there is no proof content to assess. The trees are unmodified templates, and the repos say so.

*What cannot be concluded.* That any of it typechecks. Not one of trope-checker's 24 theory obligations, and not THY-1 or APP-1..APP-4 in mathesis, can be said from here to check or to fail to check. The CI receipts in both repos are the sole evidence of a kernel run, and they were read, not reproduced. Every "verified" claim in the stack rests on those receipts.

*Theorems whose stated type does not support the claim made of them.* This is the exact list, and it is the substance of the audit.

In every case `docs/status/PROOF-STATUS.adoc` carries the correct name — the human surface is right and the machine-readable ledger is the drifted artefact, which is the opposite of the usual direction and worth recording as such.

*Not defects, for the record.* The workbench citing trope-checker's Agda flags as `--safe --without-K` where the pragmas carry four flags is narrower, not wider. mathesis's `Laws` record being weaker than an ordered abelian group makes APP-2/APP-3 narrower than the theorem. Neither is reportable.

[[3-is-the-trope-stack-reflected-in-mathesis]]
== 3. Is the trope stack reflected in mathesis?

*No. Not one record.* `grep -E 'trope|haec|hermeneia|vocarium|particularity'` over `docs/atlas/claims/claims.jsonl` and `bridges.jsonl` returns nothing. `claims.jsonl` holds 22 records from exactly three repositories (hyperpolymath/secret-types N-1..N-9, metadatastician/lintel L-1..L-7 and A-1, hyperpolymath/mathesis THY-1 and APP-1..APP-4); `bridges.jsonl` holds two, neither touching the stack.

*The precondition is already met.* The stack is in the census frame and public: `repos.jsonl:187` haec, `:189` hermeneia, `:359` trope-checker, `:360` trope-particularity-workbench, `:382` vocarium. `rows.jsonl` has census rows for haec (:101), hermeneia (:103) and trope-checker (:200, class "theory", 514 declarations, "agda 10, lean 12, idris2 16"); vocarium and the workbench have no row.

*The exact mechanical gap.* The _schema_ is satisfiable: a row pinning `{"path":"verification/pwg.json","sha256":<64 hex>}` passes `validate()`. What cannot happen is the _derivation_ — such a row derives `ungraded` carrying the reader's refusal. Feeding `pwg.json` to the pinned `readProofReceipt` yields `LintError: "line 1: line is 26347 octets; a deed line is at most 998 octets"`. Item by item, what is missing:

* it is JSON, not a `(praxis-deed …)` S-expression with `:schema-version`, `:canonical-name`, `:profile proof-receipt`, `:repository`, `:source-commit` (pwg.json's top-level keys are `commit, note, policy, receipts, repo, schema_version, theorems`; no `:source-commit`);
* the filename does not dispatch as a deed — accepted empirically as `trope_praxis.deed`, refused as `pwg.json` ("filename 'pwg.json' matches no deed dispatch pattern");
* its tokens are `PROVEN` / `PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS` / `OPEN`; the reader's closed §5 vocabulary is `["proven","proven-with-postulates","tested-only","argued","open"]`;
* its Agda legs use route `re-proved`; the reader fails any claim where `(prover === "agda") !== (route === "source")`;
* each receipt's `inputs_digest` is a 40-hex git tree OID; the reader demands `:inputs-sha256` as 64 lowercase hex, recomputed from an `(inputs-manifest (file :path … :sha256 …) …)` clause pwg.json has no analogue of;
* its axioms are bare name lists; the reader demands `(axioms (axiom :name … :disposition b|c|debt))` and counts a witness only when every disposition is `c`.

There is no `verification/receipts/` directory in trope-checker on either the branch or `origin/main`, and none of haec, hermeneia, vocarium or the workbench holds any receipt file.

*Cataloguing it would buy visibility, not grade.* trope-checker's Lean legs are state `BRIDGE-PENDING` (not RECONCILED), its Rocq legs are route `unavailable`, and the ledger itself records that the Lean package imports Mathlib, "which PROOF-POLICY §2 forbids for a recheck leg". The ceiling is therefore PWG-3 for the seventeen and ungraded for the seven — exactly trope-checker's own published census.

*Nobody owns the work.* `grep` over `docs/status/ROADMAP.adoc` for `trope|haec|hermeneia|vocarium` returns zero hits. The only assignment that reaches trope-checker is indirect: WO 5.1 (`docs/proposals/mathesis-re-anchoring.adoc:1065-1069`) tells a campaign to start with "the repositories that the registry and the register already name", and the register pointer names `hyperpolymath/trope-checker` at `CROSS-PROVER-RECONCILIATION.adoc:53`. Stage 5 is "Declared" (`ROADMAP.adoc:288-291`) with no owner and no date, and haec, hermeneia, vocarium and the workbench appear nowhere in mathesis's plan.

*Where the work actually sits: in trope-checker.* mathesis needs no code change to catalogue the stack. trope-checker must emit an §8.5.1 praxis-deed receipt — per-prover inputs manifests with per-file SHA-256, axiom dispositions, lowercase tokens, Agda route `source` — which `pwg-derive.sh` does not produce.
