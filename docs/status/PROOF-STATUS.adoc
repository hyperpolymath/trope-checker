| On the unquotiented `GradeR` model, an element is left-cancellative iff it is stem:[\varepsilon]: `Present` and `Atten(Q 0)` are distinct collision witnesses. 3+^| _Factorisation_

| O1-carrier (atom factorisation)
| *Mechanised:* every grade is the stem:[\triangleright]-product of its six single-slot projections, on the ratified `GradeR` (`EffectAtomsRatified.lean`). *Mechanised separately,* over the coordinate value types `Fate`/`Bond`/`Merge` — which `GradeR` reuses unchanged, so the results are independent of either carrier (`EffectAtoms.lean`): the nine-label type has nine elements (`nine_families`, the cardinality of `AtomFamily`), each label is realised by some coordinate value, and the three classifiers' images are pairwise disjoint. *Not mechanised:* the link between the two. No theorem maps a grade's slot projections to `AtomFamily`, and the `emb*_one` lemmas that would begin that bridge are stated but used by nothing; so "the projections are drawn from the nine families" and "the typology is exhaustive" are argued in the module docstring, not stated as propositions. Exhaustiveness over the coordinate types holds by their being closed inductives and by the totality of the three classifiers, which no proposition records. `EffectAtoms.lean`'s `Grade.atom_factorization` is the factorisation statement over the historical pre-ratification carrier, retained as history (`spec/calculus.adoc §3.5`, `§10`).
| Lean `EffectAtomsRatified.lean`: `Trope.GradeR.atom_factorization`; `EffectAtoms.lean`: `Trope.nine_families`, `Trope.family_realized`, `Trope.families_disjoint`, `fateFamily_none_iff`. | Tropical factorisation, (B′) sighted
| A nontrivial surjective monoid homomorphism stem:[\pi_R] onto the commutative tropical fidelity carrier exists and distinguishes `Dropped` (stem:[\infty]) from `Falsified` (stem:[\top]); the verbatim-payload map is not a homomorphism; and any veridicality-sighted homomorphism must send `atten Unknown` *away from* the deceptive top, not onto it — `sighted_forces_squash` concludes `f (atten Delta.unknown) ≠ Delta.unknown` from multiplicativity plus `f dropped ≠ f falsified`. The repair used here is exactly that move: `squash` sends the deceptive top to the honest one (`squash_top : squash Delta.unknown = Delta.total`, never back to it by `squash_ne_unknown`) and is itself additive (`squash_add`).

| Target 1 — intrinsic graded calculus
| The intrinsic typing judgement encoded by `TB.Syntax` over the ratified grade monoid, with the F4 tiers as closures. This result covers only that encoded calculus; `fuse`, `fix`, and per-field presence tracking are omitted and remain open.
| Agda `TB.Syntax`, `TB.Grade`, `TB.Tier`.

| Target 2 — grade-exact substitution
| Cut preserves the grade exactly; grade commutes with cut.
| Agda `TB.Substitution.{cut-preserves-grade,cut-grade-comm}`.

| Target 3 — subject reduction, β/let core
| β and `let` steps preserve type and grade exactly; the redex grade is the contractum grade; the tier is preserved; sequencing two core computations stays in the core. The fragment is narrow and intrinsic: `TB.Reduction._↝_` has exactly two constructors, `β-app` and `β-let`, each requiring the substituted argument at grade `ε`, with no congruence or evaluation-context rule and no step for any effect former. Because the relation is indexed at the same type *and* the same grade on both sides, preservation holds by construction rather than by induction over a reduction system, as the module header states. There is no progress theorem.
| Agda `TB.Reduction.{β-app,β-let,let-redex-grade,let-tier-preserved,seq-stays-core}`. (The `fix` case is open: see CO-1 below.)

| Target 4 — Honest Generation and Veridicality
| Honest grades are closed under composition (`TB.Honest.honest-closed-▷`); the unit and writable effect atoms in `TB.Honest` are honest. This is a grade-level closure result; it does not establish that every typed term has an honest grade.
| Agda `TB.Honest`.

== Proof Witness Grades

Estate policy
link:https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/blob/main/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc[`PROOF-POLICY.adoc`]
§8 (owner ruling 2026-11-04) requires each *theory* theorem's Proof Witness
Grade to be recorded in its home repository's proof-status file (§8.5). This
section is that record. PWG counts independent machine-checked witnesses for a
theory theorem; *lower is stronger* (§8, the numbers count down). It is not a
status token (§5) and not a statement that the theorem complies with the policy
(§8.4 rule 1).

The evidence lives in `verification/pwg.json`: per obligation, each leg's route,
its §5 token, its §2.2 correspondence state and its receipts. **No grade is
written there.** §8.4 rule 2 says a grade is computed from the receipts and the
§2.2 records, and that "a grade written by hand that the receipts do not support
is drift" — so `scripts/pwg-derive.sh` is the only thing that turns evidence into
a grade, and `scripts/pwg-check.sh` re-derives on every run and fails if this
table, or the census below, is not what the evidence yields. A `pwg` key in the
ledger is itself a gate failure.

Per §8.1 the ladder covers theory obligations only. The Idris2 developments are
engineering restatements: the ledger records them under `reference`, never as a
leg, so they can neither raise nor lower a grade.

=== Mathlib, and what the owner ruled

Every Lean module here sits in the `import Mathlib` cone (`GradeBoundary.lean:3`,
pinned in `lakefile.toml`), which PROOF-POLICY §2 forbids: "No Mathlib, in Lean or
elsewhere … A proof that needs a large external library to compile is a
dependency, not a proof the estate owns." ADR 0005 D4 meanwhile made "Lean 4 +
Mathlib" this repository's *provenance* prover, a role §1 does not define.

Owner ruling 2026-11-09: **Lean is a generator, not a leg.** The Lean development
keeps Mathlib and keeps its purpose — finding and stress-testing theorems — and
its results are then restated in core Agda, which is what becomes gradeable. The
Mathlib cone therefore never enters the trusted base, and no Lean row here is or
will be a §2 recheck leg. The ledger records every Lean leg of the Mathlib
development as `route: re-proved, state: BRIDGE-PENDING`, which does not count
(§2.2, §8.2). (The core-Lean legs added on 2026-11-17 for the seven order and L4
rows import nothing, Mathlib included, and are recorded `RECONCILED`. Whether this
ruling also covers them is put to the owner: see <<recheck-legs>>.) The
two Lean-only obligations — the O1 atom factorisation and the tropical (B′)
sighting — stay `ungraded` until an Agda statement of them exists at all; tier-2
membership has its Agda witnesses already and stays `ungraded` only until the
exhaustive step joins them. The census at the head of this section is the
authoritative split.
The amendment to ADR 0005 D4 implementing this is tracked separately; until it
lands, the ADR and the policy disagree on paper and this paragraph is the
operative reading.

== Modelling notes

Faithful-but-simplified choices, recorded for honesty:

* *Provenance tags on `Fused` are erased* in the verified core (they are metadata
  that does not affect the lattice laws); the executable checker carries them.
* *Deceptive duals are modelled as each coordinate's bottom* (worst retention,
  absorbing), which is how the calculus orders `Conflated`; this makes every
  honest floor fail against a deceptive grade (HC-2) by construction.
* *L2 is stated with stem:[\varepsilon]'s literal value* in Idris (a bare top-level
  `epsilon` as a function argument does not reduce under the unifier); since
  `epsilon = MkGrade Present Present Present Present Intact Single`
  definitionally, the statement is stem:[\varepsilon \triangleright g = g].
* *The use-model floor is a partial per-coordinate demand, not a grade value.*
  HC-4 (the Σ-coherence: bond⊣bearer-fate) is enforced on edge grade VALUES, where
  it makes incoherent states unrepresentable; the floor is a set of independent
  minimum-demands (each coordinate optional), so an author CAN write an incoherent
  floor (e.g. bearer-Dropped + bond-Intact). This is harmless — each demanded
  coordinate is checked independently — but is an author-error surface, not
  covered by the HC-4 unrepresentability claim. (Audit 2026-06-22.)
