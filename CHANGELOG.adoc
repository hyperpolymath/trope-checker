==== Changed

* *Proof Witness Grades recorded, and the proof records reconciled with the
sources (2026-11-09).* `+docs/status/PROOF-STATUS.adoc+` gains the §8.5 PWG record
that `+PROOF-POLICY.adoc+` §8 (owner ruling 2026-11-04) requires, with both theory
groups derived as `+ungraded+` — Theorem B for want of a §2.1-conformant *receipt*
(its flags in force already are §2.1), the Lean-witnessed results for want of an
Agda *source* — plus the §1.1 import-cone record and a census in the form §8.4
rule 7 allows. Four published rows narrowed to what the provers actually state: L3
(two-sidedness is stated only for `+Falsified+` in Agda), L4 (Agda covers fate and
fidelity; Lean covers fate; bond, merge and the grade-level lifting have an Idris2
leg only, which §8.1 excludes from the ladder), Target 3 (`+_↝_+` has two
ε-argument rules, no congruence, preservation definitional, no progress theorem),
and C3 (now cites the ratified `+FateA.dropped_still_not_cancel+`; the
pre-ratification `+dropped_absorbing+` is marked history). The README no longer
attributes the atom and tropical factorisations to Agda and Idris2 — they are
Lean-only. The O1-carrier evidence cell cites the emitted axiom lines and names its
receipt's commit instead of the inert in-source `+#print axioms+` directive, with
the carry-forward from `+4ecba4f+` stated and the proof trees shown identical. The
history section no longer reads as if O1 itself were closed.
* *"Axiom-clean" was an overclaim, and two neighbouring table rows were stale
(2026-11-09).* `+README.adoc+`'s layout table called the Lean grade-boundary
package "sorry-free, axiom-clean", and `+docs/proof-debt.adoc+`'s standing
position said the Idris2 core, the Lean ground truth and the Agda metatheory
"are all axiom-clean for their claimed lemmas". The kernel reports `+propext+`
and `+Quot.sound+`, and `+Classical.choice+` in three theorems, so both now use
the wording already adopted in `+PROOF-STATUS.adoc+` — no `+sorryAx+`, no user
axiom, resting on those three — which is what PROOF-POLICY §8.4 rule 6 requires
to travel with the claim. In the same table, the grade-factorisation verdict
`+(B)+` now says "on the *pre-ratification* carrier" and points at `+(B′)+` and
`+GradeFactorisationR.lean+`, and the Theorem B row names the full §2.1
invocation rather than `+--safe --without-K+`.
* *Theorem B reaches `+PWG-3+`, the estate's first graded theory theorem
(2026-11-09).* It was recorded as `+ungraded+` earlier the same day, for want of
a receipt rather than a proof: the flags in force already were the PROOF-POLICY
§2.1 invocation, but every written-up Agda result here said
`+--safe --without-K+` only, and §2.1 declassifies prior successes under that
profile. Dispatching `+proofs.yml+` on `+main+` supplied the missing record —
run 42623339243 at `+c0eb4f1+`, log ending `+PASS: All.agda + Smoke.agda
typecheck (--safe --without-K --cubical-compatible; --safe => postulate-free by
construction)+`, with Agda 2.7.0.1 and `+agda-stdlib+` v2.0 pinned by SHA-256
and the §1.1 import cone now recorded. Nothing about the mathematics changed.
`+PWG-3+` is not a discharge (§8.4 rule 1): §2's recheck obligation is owed in
full and is unreachable until a Rocq development exists. The same run was green
on both Lean packages at the current commit, so every receipt in this file now
names `+c0eb4f1+` and no row carries a carry-forward.
* *The (B′) tropical-factorisation row stated its own theorem backwards
(2026-11-09).* It read "any sighted homomorphism squashes `+Unknown+` to the
deceptive top". `+Unknown+` *is* the deceptive top of the fidelity carrier, and
`+sighted_forces_squash+` concludes the opposite — `+f (atten Delta.unknown) ≠
Delta.unknown+` — i.e. a sighted homomorphism must send it *away* from that
value. The row now states the theorem, and names the repair that performs the
move (`+squash_top+`, `+squash_ne_unknown+`, `+squash_add+`). The row and the
PWG Lean leg also now name their receipts, as the O1-carrier row already did.
* *`+Trope.Order+` no longer cites Python as the provenance of proof content
(2026-11-09).* Two transitivity lemmas' docstrings read "exhaustive;
Python-verified", naming a language on the estate deny-list as what checked a
proof. What actually enforces exhaustiveness is Idris2's coverage checker under
`+%default total+`, where a missing case is a compile error; the docstrings now
say that. Comments only; no proof term changed.
* *Theorem B interface report brought back to the ratified carrier (2026-11-09).*
`+verification/proofs/agda/theorem-b/INTERFACE-REPORT.adoc+` stated
`+dropped ▷f falsified = dropped+` as the live tier-homomorphism-failure witness.
Amendment (A1) reversed that equation; the report now gives the witness the Agda
actually proves (`+dropped ▷f atten unknown+` against the tier meet) and no longer
claims the retired `+fate-L4-fails+` is a compiled guard theorem. Target 2's verdict
no longer advertises a sharpness refutation that ratification retired, the
toolchain and spec pointers match the pins (Agda 2.7.0.1, stdlib v2.0, spec v0.2),
and the `+fix+` obstruction count is one, not two, per ADR 0004.
* *Local Lean source gate strengthened to match CI (2026-11-09).*
`+verification/proofs/lean4/grade-boundary/justfile+`'s `+falsify+` recipe grepped
raw source and matched `+axiom+` only at line start, so it missed
`+private axiom foo+` and tripped on the honest "sorry-free" prose in the module

regexes as `+.github/workflows/proofs.yml+`. Demonstrated on a planted probe: the
old form missed the private axiom and false-positived on comment prose; the new
form catches the axiom, ignores the prose, and still catches a real `+sorry+`.

==== Added

* *ADR 0005* (`+docs/decisions/0005-v0.2-consolidation-o5-and-prover-roles.adoc+`,
Proposed): spec v0.2 normative; O5 proposed for adoption from the workbench proposal of
2026-07-17; O6 opened; prover roles (Agda kernel, Idris2 reference, Lean
provenance, Rust fast core); templates removed; the carrier-as-parameter question
(D6) recorded, not decided.

* *Grade-algebra ground truth (Lean 4 + Mathlib).*
`+verification/proofs/lean4/grade-boundary/+`: a sorry-free, axiom-clean
(`+propext+`/`+Quot.sound+` only) formalisation of the honest/deceptive
cancellation boundary — the non-commutative product monoid, full-carrier
conicality, and the three-tier strict cancellation boundary
(cancellation only on the finite-fidelity core).
* *Theorem B — calculus metatheory (Agda, `+--safe --without-K+`, zero
postulates).* `+verification/proofs/agda/theorem-b/+`: intrinsic graded
calculus; grade-exact value substitution; subject reduction for β/let
(`+fix+`/lfp open); Honest Generation (HC-3). Records the F2 result that
symmetric cut is impossible and the F2×F4 result that tier-preservation
is closure, not a homomorphism.
* *Integration report.* `+verification/proofs/INTEGRATION-REPORT.adoc+`:
the unified R-id ↔ Lean ↔ Agda ↔ status table, the convergence analysis
(central open problem CO-1: no operational model for `+fix+`/routing,
gated by O4), and the divergence adjudication.
* *Proposal.* `+docs/proposals/panic-attack-signed-suppression.adoc+`:
an unforgeable, auto-stale signed-waiver design to replace forgeable
inline suppression markers.
* *Tropical factorisation probe (Lean 4 + Mathlib).*
`+verification/proofs/lean4/grade-factorisation/+`: imports the
grade-boundary carrier (no redefinition) and decides, purely
algebraically, whether the grade admits a homomorphic projection onto a
commutative tropical cost. Verdict *(B) factors but veridicality-blind*
— a nontrivial monoid homomorphism onto commutative `+Δ+` exists, but
every multiplicative map into any commutative monoid is forced to
identify honest `+dropped+` with deceptive `+falsified+`; the blindness
is co-located with the fate-axis non-commutativity (bond/merge deception
stays separable). Sorry-free; axiom-clean. A *precondition only* — does
not touch CO-1. Tracked next stage: issue #10.
* *Justfile recipes* `+build-lean+`, `+build-agda+`, `+verify-all+`
(`+build/just/proofs.just+`); `+build-lean+` and the `+verify-all+`
honesty gate now cover both Lean developments (grade-boundary +
grade-factorisation).
