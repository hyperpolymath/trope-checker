= ADR 0005 — Spec v0.2 consolidation, O5 proposed, O6 opened, prover roles, template removal; the carrier-as-parameter question recorded
:revdate: 2026-10-27

== Status

*Proposed — owner to accept.* Drafted in the Phase 0 pass ("make the status
surfaces true"); the spec consolidation it ratifies landed as
`docs(spec): consolidate the calculus at v0.2` (2026-10-13). Nothing here changes
a semantics; D6 is recorded, not decided. D2 and O5 remain PROPOSED until
the owner accepts this ADR; the O5 text in §10 is a proposed interface.

== Context

After ADR 0004 (R-2026-07-07) the normative text was split across four
documents written against two different carriers: `spec/calculus.adoc` v0.1
(2026-06-22) with the ratification spliced in as notes, the never-merged
addendum `spec/calculus-v0.2-draft.adoc` (2026-06-23), and the two design notes
of 2026-07-07 (the L4 boundary and atoms; the CO-1 operational model). The spec
still said "seven effects" while the vocabulary, `EffectAtoms.lean` and the IR
said nine. The third firewall ("veridicality is declared and propagated, not
checked") cited an obligation O5 that §10 never defined; the workbench proposed
the missing entry on 2026-07-17
(`trope-particularity-workbench/docs/theory/formalisms/defining-o5-veridicality-oracle.adoc`).
T-Sub's one-sidedness means the verdict certifies sufficiency only, which the
privacy direction (a ceiling on retention) cannot use. The estate's proof-kernel
ruling of 2026-10-12 fixed the role of each prover across repositories, and this
repo still described itself as a "four-prover" stack and carried three
never-instantiated Coq / TLA+ / Agda templates from the repository mint.

== Decisions

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Decision

| *D1 (one normative document)* | `spec/calculus.adoc` v0.2 (the consolidation
of 2026-10-13) is the normative text. The addendum is folded in and removed; the
two 2026-07-07 notes and `verification/proofs/INTEGRATION-REPORT.adoc` are
history and analysis, cited from the spec where they carry the argument, never
the other way round. Where any older status surface disagrees with the spec, the
spec wins.

| *D2 (O5 proposed)* | §10 gains O5, the veridicality oracle, proposed for adoption from the
workbench proposal of 2026-07-17 unchanged in substance: Remark O5-a
(IR-relative undecidability of veridicality), the oracle interface with O5-1
soundness, O5-2 attestation and O5-3 no-laundering, the falsifier, and the
upgrade path (mechanise O5-1 as a side condition on an `Oracle`-indexed verdict
judgement). Status in §10: PROPOSED pending acceptance of this ADR; mechanisation
OPEN. Vocarium's warrants are
O5-2 attestations; Hermeneia's _licensed_ band is where O5-backed claims sit.

| *D3 (O6 opened)* | §10 gains O6, the ceiling verdict: the dual theorem
stem:[\rho \sqsubseteq g_{\mathrm{ceiling}}] that a "no more than this may be
retained" use-model needs, which T-Sub's downward-only weakening does not give for
conservative lowering. Deliverable: the dual verdict proved for the exact-lowering
fragment and stated as unavailable for the conservative one, or a second,
upper-bound grade on edges. Status: OPEN.

| *D4 (prover roles)* | The estate kernel ruling of 2026-10-12 applied here
(§9.3): *Agda* is the normative kernel (Theorem B; CO-1 mechanisation targets it);
*Idris2* is the executable reference (the verified core and the reference
`tropecheck`); *Lean 4 + Mathlib* is provenance (the grade-algebra boundary, the
atom and tropical factorisations, kept with their axiom audits); *Rust* is the
fast core, validated against the reference over the conformance corpus.
Consequence: `docs/status/PROOF-STATUS.adoc` leads with Agda, and new
metatheory goes to Agda unless an ADR says otherwise.

| *D5 (three provers; templates removed)* | `verification/proofs/coq/TypeSafety.v`,
`verification/proofs/tlaplus/StateMachine.tla` and
`verification/proofs/agda/Properties.agda` — mint templates whose own text says
"Replace with your project's …", never instantiated — are removed, with their
tree and recipe references. The repository describes itself as three provers
(Agda, Idris2, Lean) plus a Rust fast core.

| *D6 (recorded, not decided)* | Whether the fidelity carrier should be a
*parameter* — any resource algebra with two adjoined tops, instantiated from
`hyperpolymath/resource-algebra` — rather than the one instance
stem:[\mathbb{N}\cup\{\infty,\top\}]. This would answer the "uncalibrated
ordinal arithmetic" objection (the finite part of the carrier adds losses whose
unit is unstated) by making the unit the instance's business. O4 is RESOLVED for
the instance (ADR 0004); the parameterisation is Phase 1A work and is *not*
decided by this ADR.
|===

== Consequences

* One place to read the calculus; "nine effects" throughout, reconciled with
  "seven" as nine atom families of which six are writable and three are faults.
* The third firewall cites the proposed O5 obligation. O5 is dischargeable in
  principle (mechanise O5-1) and refutable (its falsifier is stated).
* The privacy direction has a named obligation (O6) instead of an unstated gap;
  the verdict's one-sidedness is written down as a limitation, not hidden.
* Status surfaces (`PROOF-STATUS`, `ROADMAP`, `AFFIRMATION`, the Agda `fix`
  footer) are rewritten to the ratified world in the same pass; the Lean
  honesty gate and `verify-all` cover every tracked Lean source.
* `just proof-check-all` no longer invokes the Coq recipe; `just proof-status`
  reports three provers. The historical mention of the templates in
  `INTEGRATION-REPORT.adoc` stays as the record of what was there.
* D6 leaves the carrier an instance; downstream (vocarium, hermeneia) may keep
  reading `Attenuated(δ)` with stem:[\delta \in \mathbb{N}].

== Rejected alternatives

* *Keep the addendum as a separate v0.2 document* — rejected: two normative
  texts on two carriers is how readers and agents kept landing in the
  pre-ratification world.
* *"Four-prover" wording, Rust as a prover* — rejected: the fast core proves
  nothing; it is validated against the reference. Naming it a prover overclaims.
* *Keep the Coq / TLA+ / Agda templates as future placeholders* — rejected: they
  were never instantiated, each advertises a prover the roles ruling does not
  assign here, and `proof-check-coq` skipping silently is a gate that cannot fail.
* *Decide D6 now (make the carrier a parameter in this pass)* — rejected: it
  changes the carrier every proof development is stated over; it belongs to
  Phase 1A with `resource-algebra` as the instance supplier, after CO-1
  mechanisation lands on the fixed instance.
* *Leave O5 as the workbench proposal* — rejected: an obligation cited by the
  checker's firewalls must live in the checker's spec, not in a proposal
  downstream cannot cite as normative.
